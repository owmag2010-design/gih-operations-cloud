# Standard Fee Schedule (South Africa)

Source: Owen's fee schedule, 20 Oct 2026. Configuration data, loaded into the `services` table for Veritas Emigrate and Mimshac Group by `npm run db:seed`.

Rules: **Total = core fees only.** Add-ons are charged on top, only when the client needs them. These are always add-ons: assisted submission, MIE, board, SAQA, courier, and anything the schedule marks "Add on". Deposit = 50% of the total. Consultations are paid in full.
